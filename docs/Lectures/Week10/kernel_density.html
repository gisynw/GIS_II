Simple Kernel Density Demo

This simplified demo uses only four points. The left panel shows a 2D continuous density surface, and the right panel shows a 3D kernel function that is highest at the center and decreases toward the boundary.
Larger bandwidth creates a smoother, broader hotspot. Smaller bandwidth makes the hotspot more local.
Increase this to make the kernel hill taller in the 3D view.
What students should notice
• Each point spreads influence to nearby space.
• Overlapping influence creates a stronger hotspot.
• The kernel is highest at the point center and becomes smaller toward the edge.
Key idea
points → kernel spread → continuous hotspot surface
