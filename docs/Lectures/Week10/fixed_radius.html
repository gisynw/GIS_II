Fixed Radius Interpolation Demo

This demo uses sample points with clustered and sparse areas. Students can adjust the fixed radius, then click Generate Surface to see how radius influences the interpolated surface.
Small radius = more local detail, but more empty cells. Large radius = smoother surface.
How to use
1. Look at the sample points.
2. Adjust the radius.
3. Click Generate Surface.
4. Compare what happens in dense areas and sparse areas.
Guiding questions
• What happens when the radius is too small?
• Why do dense point areas get values more easily?
• What changes when the radius becomes larger?
• Click a cell on the map: which sample points fall inside that cell's search radius?
