Spline Interpolation: From Stations to DEM

Drag the figure to rotate the 3D view. This demo shows how a few elevation stations on a hillside can be connected into a smooth spline-like surface to create a continuous DEM.

Controls

1 = hillside base, 2 = add elevation stations, 3 = generate smooth spline-like DEM surface.
Higher smoothness creates a gentler, more generalized surface.
Drag on the figure to rotate. Use zoom if labels or surface feel too small.
What students should notice
Step 1 shows the hillside before sample measurements are added.
Interaction
• Click and drag to rotate the 3D view.
• Compare Step 2 and Step 3 from different angles.
• Notice how the smooth surface passes exactly through the measured stations.
Key idea
Known elevation points → smooth surface → DEM
